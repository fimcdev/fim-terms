## **3. Token Supply**

### **3.1 Maximum Supply**

The maximum total supply of FIM is fixed at **10,000,000,000 (ten billion) tokens**.

### **3.2 No Inflation and No Additional Minting**

The Project states that the token supply cannot increase because minting authority and freeze authority are permanently revoked (see Section 14 and Section 46 for technical references). No new FIM tokens can be created under any circumstances.

### **3.3 Token Burns**

The Project may, at its sole discretion, burn tokens that are unused, unallocated, unsold, or otherwise not distributed, including but not limited to:

* ICO tokens from the sale allocation that remain unsold after the ICO closes;
* any portion of the scaling liquidity allocation (Sections 3.5 and 8.7) not required to fund the initial pool;
* airdrop allocations that remain unclaimed after the claim deadline; and
* any other allocations reserved for incentives that are not ultimately used.

Because both the sale allocation and the liquidity allocation scale with the quantity of FIM actually sold, the quantity burned increases when less is sold. The Project intends to execute the burn on-chain in a verifiable manner following the close of the ICO and the funding of the liquidity pool.

Token burns permanently reduce total supply. A burn does not create any compensation, refund, or additional entitlement to other participants, and does not imply any promise of increased token value.

### **3.4 No Obligation to Maintain or Support Any Price**

The Project does not undertake any obligation to maintain, support, or stabilize any market price for FIM, nor to provide buybacks, redemptions, or price support mechanisms. Any market price is determined solely by market participants and trading venues, and may be volatile, illiquid, or subject to manipulation by third parties.

### **3.5 Token Allocation**

The fixed total supply of 10,000,000,000 FIM is allocated as follows:

| Allocation | Amount | Share |
|------------|--------|-------|
| ICO (public sale, Section 5) | up to 4,700,000,000 FIM | up to 47% |
| Liquidity provisioning (Section 8) | scales with tokens sold (0.90 × sold) | up to ~42% |
| Airdrop & community incentives (Section 4) | 500,000,000 FIM | 5% |
| Project Reserve | 500,000,000 FIM | 5% |
| Burn (unsold / unused, Section 3.3) | remainder | variable |
| **Total** | **10,000,000,000 FIM** | **100%** |

The FIM allocated to liquidity provisioning is **not a fixed amount** but scales with the quantity actually sold in the ICO, calculated as **0.90 × (FIM sold)**. This ensures the initial liquidity pool opens at the ICO reference price regardless of how much is sold (see Section 8.7). Any FIM from the ICO allocation that is not sold, and any liquidity FIM not required, is permanently burned as described in Section 3.3, such that the fixed total supply of 10,000,000,000 FIM is always fully accounted for.

The **Project Reserve** (500,000,000 FIM) is held by the Project for operational, ecosystem, infrastructure, security, and long-term sustainability purposes. Use of the Project Reserve is at the Project's sole discretion, subject to applicable law and the principles set out in these Terms.

Any allocation amounts that are not ultimately used (including unclaimed airdrop tokens or undistributed ICO tokens) may be permanently burned or reallocated as described in Sections 3.3, 34.2, and related provisions.

## **5. ICO Structure**

### **5.0 ICO Design and Fair Launch Principle**

The ICO is designed around a single principle: the initial liquidity pool should open at approximately the ICO reference price, so that no participant — whether early or late — receives a structural pricing advantage over another at launch.

To give effect to this principle:

* the liquidity allocation **scales with the quantity of FIM actually sold** (0.90 × sold), so the pool opens at approximately the reference price regardless of how much is sold (Section 8.7);
* FIM sold in the ICO is delivered **in full** to participants' wallets, and the liquidity FIM is a **separate allocation** that remains in the pool (Section 8.7); and
* any unsold or unused FIM is **permanently burned** (Section 3.3), so that no undistributed allocation accrues to the Project by default.

The maximum ICO sale quantity stated in Section 5.1 reflects the portion of the fixed total supply committed to liquidity, community, and reserve allocations under this design. It is a structural parameter and is **not** a representation, projection, or guarantee of expected demand, amount to be raised, token value, or future price. Nothing in this Section creates any entitlement or expectation of profit (see Sections 3.4, 20, and 55).

### **5.1 ICO Allocation**

Up to **4,700,000,000 (four billion seven hundred million) FIM** may be made available for sale through the Initial Coin Offering (“ICO”). A separate, scaling allocation of FIM is reserved for liquidity provisioning as described in Section 8, calculated as 0.90 × the quantity of FIM actually sold. FIM sold in the ICO is delivered to participants' wallets; the liquidity allocation is a distinct portion used to fund the initial trading pool and is not delivered to ICO participants (see Section 8.7).

Any contributed SOL used for operational purposes during the ICO period pursuant to Section 7.2.1 shall be counted toward, and deducted from, the 10% operational allocation described in this Section, such that total combined operational use does not exceed 10% of net ICO proceeds and the 90% allocation to liquidity provisioning remains unaffected in full.

### **8.2 Pool Capital Wallet (PCW)**

The PCW is a dedicated wallet used for liquidity provisioning operations. Funds in the PCW are used solely to add liquidity to official pools (e.g., Raydium, Meteora) according to the Project’s publicly disclosed plan. The Project may publish transaction links (tx hashes) to improve transparency.

### **8.3 No Premature Trading Promise**

Prior to the official addition of liquidity to the relevant pool(s), trading may be impractical, illiquid, unavailable, or subject to third-party behavior. The Project does not promise that any secondary market will exist at any time, and does not guarantee that trading can occur before liquidity is actually added.

### **8.4 Liquidity Timing & Fair Launch Policy**

Liquidity will be added **after token distribution is completed**. The exact timing of initial liquidity addition is **intentionally not disclosed in advance**.

This approach is used to:

* support fair and orderly market conditions,
* reduce automated exploitation (bots, sniping, MEV),
* prevent unfair advantages based on advance timing knowledge, and
* protect community participants from predictable launch-time manipulation.

The Project does not guarantee any specific launch time, price behavior, liquidity depth, or immediate market activity after liquidity is added.

### **8.5 Progressive Additions and Transparency**

Liquidity may be added in one or multiple transactions. Additions may occur progressively based on operational readiness, safety considerations, market conditions, or technical constraints. Where possible, the Project may publish transaction links via official channels or a transparency page.

### **8.6 LP Token Locking and Release**

Liquidity provider (LP) tokens generated from liquidity provisioning funded by ICO proceeds are intended to be locked for a **36-month cliff**, followed by a **1/24 linear monthly release** thereafter.

Unlocked LP tokens may be used for purposes such as:

* reinforcing or rebalancing liquidity,
* supporting ecosystem development,
* responding to security incidents,
* adapting to changes in market structure or DEX conditions.

All such actions are expected to be on-chain and transparent where reasonably possible, but the Project does not guarantee publication timing in all circumstances.

### **8.7 Liquidity Pool Composition and Opening Price**

The initial liquidity pool is composed of two sides funded from distinct sources:

* the **SOL side**, funded by 90% of the net SOL proceeds raised in the ICO (Section 8.1); and
* the **FIM side**, funded by a dedicated liquidity allocation calculated as **0.90 × the quantity of FIM sold** in the ICO (Sections 3.5 and 5.1).

Because both sides of the pool are derived proportionally from the same quantity of FIM sold and SOL raised, the pool's initial price is designed to open at approximately the ICO reference price. This is a structural property of the scaling mechanism and is intended to reduce launch-time price dislocation, discourage predatory arbitrage against ICO participants, and support an orderly opening. It is not a guarantee of any price, and once trading begins the market price is determined solely by market participants (Sections 3.4 and 21).

FIM used to fund the FIM side of the pool is a **separate allocation** from FIM sold to ICO participants. ICO participants receive the full quantity of FIM they purchased delivered to their wallets; the liquidity FIM remains in the pool as tradable liquidity and is not delivered to participants. Any FIM from the ICO allocation that is not sold, and any liquidity FIM not required to fund the pool, is permanently burned pursuant to Section 3.3, so that the fixed total supply of 10,000,000,000 FIM remains fully accounted for at all times.
