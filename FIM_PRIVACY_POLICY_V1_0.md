## 4. Principles of Data Processing

FinnishMark aims to follow the following data protection principles:

- **Data minimization:** Only data reasonably necessary for operation, security, abuse prevention, eligibility control, and compliance is collected
- **Purpose limitation:** Data is processed only for explicit and legitimate operational, security, and compliance purposes
- **Security by design:** Appropriate technical and organizational safeguards are applied
- **Transparency:** Processing purposes are described publicly in this Policy and related documentation
- **No data monetization:** Personal data is never sold and is not shared for behavioral advertising purposes

## 5. Categories of Data Collected

### 5.1 Personal Data

The Project may collect the following limited personal data:

- **Email address**
  Used for account verification, essential communications, login flows, transaction-related notices, and support.

- **Wallet address (public blockchain address)**
  Used to associate eligibility for ICO participation, airdrops, claims, referral rewards, and verification records, and to verify on-chain interactions connected to the Services.

- **IP address and region-related network signals**
  Collected during registration, verification, login, and participation for security, fraud-prevention, geoblocking, jurisdiction-level restriction, compliance-support, and access-restriction purposes.

- **Verification and payment status metadata**
  Used to confirm successful human verification, payment verification, elected verification method, eligibility state, compliance screening state (where such screening is in use), and anti-abuse checks.

- **Verification transaction metadata**
  May include Stripe-related verification payment status, SOL verification payment status, wallet-signing or wallet-control proof data, transaction hashes, timestamps, quoted and confirmed verification amounts, and related operational metadata necessary to verify that the participant completed the chosen verification flow.

- **Device, browser, and session-related technical data**
  May include user-agent information, browser characteristics, approximate region inferred from network signals, timestamps, session identifiers, and similar technical metadata used for platform integrity and fraud prevention.

- **Referral, campaign, and participation metadata**
  Used to determine whether a user has qualified for referral rewards, promotional rewards, airdrops, or claims, and to detect abuse, duplication, or manipulation.

FinnishMark does **not** intentionally request or require names, postal addresses, phone numbers, government-issued identifiers, or special-category / sensitive personal data in the ordinary course of the Services.

### 5.2 Public Blockchain Data

Public wallet addresses, transaction hashes, token transfers, verification-related on-chain payments, and related blockchain activity may be visible on public block explorers or through the Solana blockchain itself. Such information is generally public and immutable by nature and may not be capable of deletion or modification by the Project.

IP addresses and related technical signals may be collected during registration, verification, login, and participation for security and fraud-prevention purposes.

IP and related technical data may be used to:

- detect automated, bot-driven, or scripted activity
- prevent multiple account or wallet abuse

- maintain platform integrity, availability, and security

IP-related data is not used for behavioral advertising or unrelated profiling.

**Indicative retention period:** Up to 24 months, after which such data may be deleted, aggregated, anonymized, or retained longer where reasonably necessary for legal claims, compliance, fraud investigations, sanctions reviews, or security incidents.
