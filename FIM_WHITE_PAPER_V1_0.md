### D.2 Token Allocation

The total fixed supply of 10,000,000,000 FIM is allocated as follows:

| Allocation | Amount | Share |
|------------|--------|-------|
| ICO (public sale) | up to 4,700,000,000 FIM | up to 47% |
| Liquidity provisioning | scales with sold (0.90 × sold) | up to ~42% |
| Airdrop & community incentives | 500,000,000 FIM | 5% |
| Project Reserve | 500,000,000 FIM | 5% |
| Burn (unsold / unused) | remainder | variable |
| **Total** | **10,000,000,000 FIM** | **100%** |

The FIM allocated to liquidity provisioning is **not a fixed amount**; it scales with the quantity actually sold, calculated as **0.90 × (FIM sold)**. This design causes the initial liquidity pool to open at approximately the ICO reference price regardless of how much is sold. FIM sold in the ICO is delivered to participants' wallets; the liquidity FIM is a separate allocation that remains in the pool as tradable liquidity and is not delivered to participants. Any unsold ICO FIM and any liquidity FIM not required is permanently burned, so the fixed supply of 10,000,000,000 FIM is always fully accounted for. See Terms & Conditions Sections 3.5, 8.7, and 3.3.

The **Project Reserve** (500,000,000 FIM) is held by the project for operational, ecosystem, infrastructure, security, and long-term sustainability purposes, subject to the discretion of the project and the principles set out in the Terms & Conditions.

Any allocation amounts that are not ultimately used (including unclaimed airdrop tokens or unsold ICO tokens) may be permanently burned or reallocated as described in the Terms & Conditions (Sections 3.3, 34.2, and related provisions).

## Part K — ICO Structure

### Fair Launch Design

The FIM ICO is structured around a single principle: the trading pool should open at the same price ICO participants paid, so that no participant — early or late, insider or public — receives a structural advantage over another at launch.

This is achieved through three deliberate design choices:

**1. Opening price equals ICO price ($0.01).** The liquidity pool is funded so that trading opens at approximately the ICO reference price. There is no "launch gap" in which insiders buy cheaply and sell into public demand, and no artificial pump. A participant who sells immediately at launch receives approximately what they paid, which removes the incentive for predatory front-running and bot sniping.

**2. Scaling liquidity, not a fixed amount.** The FIM side of the pool scales with how much is actually sold (0.90 × sold). This keeps the opening price at approximately $0.01 whether the ICO sells 500 million or the full 4.7 billion FIM. The launch is designed to be fair at any level of demand, not only if a specific target is reached.

**3. Burn what is not used.** Because both the sale and liquidity allocations scale with demand, unsold and unused FIM is permanently burned. No undistributed allocation accrues to the Project by default, and when less is sold, more is burned — so the circulating supply reflects genuine demand.

Consequently, the maximum ICO sale is capped at 4.7 billion FIM: the remaining supply is committed to liquidity, community (airdrop), reserve, or burn. This cap is not a demand forecast or a value promise — it is the mathematical consequence of guaranteeing a fair opening price under a fixed 10 billion total supply. Nothing in this design constitutes a promise of profit, value, or future price; see the Risk Factors (Part I) and the Terms & Conditions (Sections 3.4, 20, and 55).
